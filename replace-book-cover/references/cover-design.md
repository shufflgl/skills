# Cover design brief

Use this checklist to turn research into an image prompt.

## Research synthesis

Write down:

- exact display title, subtitle, and author or editor name;
- language and expected reading direction;
- genre and audience, including whether the work is fiction or nonfiction;
- central subject, emotional register, setting, period, and two or three concrete motifs;
- visual clichés to avoid and edition-specific facts that must remain accurate.

Do not summarize the entire plot. Select one governing idea and one visual metaphor.

## Art direction

Specify:

- portrait front-cover canvas, normally 2:3;
- a single focal concept with intentional negative space;
- medium, palette, light, texture, and period cues;
- title hierarchy, exact text, typography character, and placement;
- author hierarchy and safe margins;
- strong contrast and legibility at thumbnail size;
- no spine, back cover, 3D mockup, device, hands, price, barcode, badges, reviews, publisher marks, or extra text.

Ask for an original visual language rather than naming or imitating an artist. For public-domain classics, distinguish the historical setting from the visual style: period accuracy does not require a faux-antique cover.

## Prompt skeleton

> Create a finished, flat front cover for **[exact title]** by **[exact author]**, based on [theme, setting, tone, and motifs]. Use [original visual direction, palette, medium, composition]. Establish a clear hierarchy with the exact title “[title]” and author “[author]”; keep all text legible, correctly spelled, and inside generous safe margins. The result must read clearly as [genre/audience] at thumbnail size. Portrait 2:3. No additional words, logo, barcode, award seal, endorsement, spine, back cover, book mockup, border crop marks, hands, or background outside the cover.

If generated typography fails twice, generate text-free artwork with reserved title and author zones, then add exact typography using a local layout tool and a properly licensed font. Do not silently deliver misspelled text.
