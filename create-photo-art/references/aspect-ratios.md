# Aspect ratios

Present the ratio and its main destination. The user may choose by ratio or use case.

| Ratio | Orientation | Best suited to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1:1` | Square | Social feeds, profile grids, album-style art, and compact digital galleries |
| `4:5` | Portrait | Instagram feed posts, editorial covers, product stories, and compact posters |
| `3:4` | Portrait | Art postcards, classic editorial posters, tablets, and general-purpose portrait art |
| `2:3` | Portrait | Photographic prints, wall art, book-like posters, and standard portrait framing |
| `9:16` | Tall portrait | Stories, Reels or Shorts covers, phone wallpapers, and full-screen mobile display |
| `3:2` | Landscape | Photographic prints, editorial spreads, desktop art, and horizontal framing |
| `16:9` | Wide landscape | Presentation covers, desktop wallpapers, video thumbnails, and wide digital banners |

## Adapt the composition

- Preserve the exact selected ratio; never stretch a source image to fill it.
- For photo-integrated styles with two regions, use a top-to-bottom division for portrait and square outputs. Use a left-to-right division for landscape outputs unless the user requests another layout.
- Keep the style's intended region balance: 55/45 remains 55/45 along the division axis, and 50/50 remains equal.
- In art-only mode, use the entire canvas for the artistic composition. Rebalance negative space, typography, subject scale, and secondary elements for the destination rather than leaving an empty photographic region.
- Protect likely interface overlays in `9:16`: keep essential faces, text, and focal details away from the extreme top and bottom edges.
- Keep critical content within comfortable safe margins for print-oriented `2:3` and `3:2` outputs. Do not invent bleed or crop marks unless requested.
