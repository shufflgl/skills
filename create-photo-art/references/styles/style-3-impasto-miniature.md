# Style 3: Impasto miniature art poster

## Composition

1. Create one independent 3:4 portrait poster split into two strictly equal regions, each 50% of the height.
2. In the upper half, preserve the original photo's subject, composition, light, color, and detail. Extend the background naturally only when needed to fit the canvas; never stretch or alter the subject.
3. Redraw the same scene in the lower half as a high-quality art-poster interpretation on a warm off-white paper background. Use dimensional palette-knife oil impasto, a miniature-landscape sensibility, and fresh colors derived from the source.
4. Preserve the subject silhouette, primary elements, and spatial relationships so the lower scene remains clearly connected to the photograph while still reading as a handcrafted artistic reinterpretation.
5. Reserve a clean English-title area on the left. Reserve a compact number and short-copy area at the lower right. Derive any wording conservatively from visible content or user-provided facts, and keep it brief.
6. Keep the layout simple and make the contrast from real scene to art poster immediately legible.

## Exclusions

Do not merge multiple photos into one poster, distort or redraw the upper subject, overcrowd the lower scene, let typography dominate, or replace the tactile paint treatment with smooth digital gradients, generic filters, or plastic 3D rendering.

## Verify

Confirm the exact 3:4 ratio and 50/50 split, faithful upper photo, recognizable lower reinterpretation, warm off-white paper, visible dimensional palette-knife strokes, miniature landscape character, fresh source-related palette, left title area, lower-right number and copy area, and a clear real-scene-to-art contrast.
