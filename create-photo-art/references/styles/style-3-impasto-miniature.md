# Style 3: Impasto miniature art poster

## Creation modes

- **Photo-integrated:** pair a faithful photographic region with an equal impasto reinterpretation, using the division axis defined by the selected ratio.
- **Art-only:** omit the original photograph and extend the impasto miniature scene across the full canvas. Derive the subject and spatial relationships from the reference photo or text brief, then rebalance the title and short-copy areas for the chosen ratio.

## Composition

1. Create one independent poster at the selected aspect ratio. In photo-integrated mode, split it into two strictly equal regions along the shared ratio guidance's division axis.
2. In the photographic region, preserve the original photo's subject, composition, light, color, and detail. Extend the background naturally only when needed to fit the canvas; never stretch or alter the subject. Skip this region entirely in art-only mode.
3. Redraw the scene in the artistic region, or across the full canvas in art-only mode, as a high-quality art-poster interpretation on a warm off-white paper background. Use dimensional palette-knife oil impasto, a miniature-landscape sensibility, and fresh colors derived from the reference or brief.
4. Preserve the subject silhouette, primary elements, and spatial relationships defined by the reference or brief. When a reference photo exists, keep the art clearly connected to it while still reading as a handcrafted reinterpretation.
5. Reserve a clean English-title area on the left. Reserve a compact number and short-copy area at the lower right. Derive any wording conservatively from visible content or user-provided facts, and keep it brief.
6. Keep the layout simple. In photo-integrated mode, make the contrast from real scene to art poster immediately legible. In art-only mode, create one complete composition rather than simulating a before-and-after layout.

## Exclusions

Do not merge multiple photos into one poster, distort or redraw the upper subject, overcrowd the lower scene, let typography dominate, or replace the tactile paint treatment with smooth digital gradients, generic filters, or plastic 3D rendering.

## Verify

Confirm the selected ratio, recognizable artistic interpretation, warm off-white paper, visible dimensional palette-knife strokes, miniature landscape character, fresh coherent palette, left title area, and lower-right number and copy area. In photo-integrated mode, confirm the 50/50 split, faithful photo, and clear real-scene-to-art contrast. In art-only mode, confirm the full canvas is intentionally composed and contains no original-photo region.
